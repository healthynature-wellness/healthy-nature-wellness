How to read an herbal study

Look for the exact plant species, preparation, dose, study design, number of participants, and limitations. One small study rarely settles a question.

The difference between food and supplements

A culinary ingredient and a concentrated extract may have very different levels of active compounds, uses, and risks.

Foraging with care

Correct identification, clean growing sites, local regulations, and sustainable harvesting are essential. When in doubt, do not harvest or consume a wild plant.
