TRADITION & CONTEXT

Tradition deserves
context.

Plants have been part of human foodways, healing traditions, ceremony, and daily life for generations. We approach that knowledge with respect and careful distinctions.

Respect the roots

Herbal knowledge is shaped by place, culture, community, and generations of practice. Distinct traditions should not be treated as interchangeable.

Tradition is not a clinical trial

A long history of use can be meaningful, but it does not establish safety or effectiveness for every person or condition.

Preparation changes the plant

Tea, food, tinctures, essential oils, and concentrated extracts can differ in strength and risk. A plant name alone does not tell the whole story.

Safety is part of wisdom

Natural substances can cause side effects and interact with medicines. Ask a qualified healthcare professional before using herbs medicinally.
