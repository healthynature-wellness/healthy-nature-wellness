EVERYDAY FOUNDATIONS

Small, steady habits.
A grounded approach.

Wellbeing is shaped by many connected parts of daily life. This section explores approachable foundations—not quick fixes or promises.

Nourishment

Build meals around a variety of foods, including vegetables, fruit, whole grains, protein sources, and healthy fats. No single ingredient defines a healthy diet.

Rest & recovery

Consistent sleep routines, a calming evening environment, and attention to persistent sleep difficulties can support overall wellbeing.

Movement

Choose enjoyable movement that fits your abilities and circumstances. Walking, gardening, stretching, and strength activities can all have a place.

Stress & connection

Time outdoors, meaningful relationships, creative activities, and supportive care can contribute to resilience. Seek professional support when stress feels unmanageable.
